Frequently Asked Questions

How do I split a Canva banner into an Instagram 3-post grid?

Create a custom canvas in Canva sized 3240 × 1080 px for squares or 3240 × 1350 px for portrait posts. Design your continuous artwork across the canvas, export as PNG, and drop it into SliceMyBanner. The tool splits it into 3 equal 1080px columns ready for upload.

Why must I post Instagram slices in reverse order?

Because Instagram automatically places your newest post at the top left of your profile. To make your banner align properly from left to right, start uploading with the bottom-right tile (Tile 3) and end with the top-left tile (Tile 1).

Is SliceMyBanner private and secure?

Yes, 100%. All image slicing and ZIP packaging happen directly inside your web browser. No photos or personal data are ever uploaded to any server.
